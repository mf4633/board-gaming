## Long description

*1,707 characters across 36 lines.*

```
Thirty-six games on your television, and a remote that actually works.

Board Gaming Hub brings the whole boardgaminghub.com catalogue to Fire TV in one
app. Open it and you land on a catalogue built for a ten-foot view: large tiles,
three rows, everything reachable with four arrows and an OK button.

THE CLASSICS
Chess with a real engine, Go, Backgammon, Othello, Mancala, Klondike Solitaire,
Mahjong, Sudoku from easy to expert, Minesweeper with no-guess boards, and 2048.

ORIGINAL STRATEGY
Agora (Mediterranean trade), Aresia (colonising Mars), Bisque (battle for the
bay), Convergence (rival civilisations on a shared economy), Odyssey, Tidelands,
Pente Grammai, and the 4,500-year-old Royal Game of Ur.

SIMULATIONS THAT RUN ON REAL FORMULAS
Built by a dam-safety engineer. Floodline is a flood-defence simulator using real
hydrology and historical floods. Bonneville Spillway Operator puts you on the
Columbia River balancing power, navigation and fish passage. Biosphere Blue is a
planet-scale climate and ecology model. Apoapsis is 3D orbital mechanics. Eclipse
Predictor draws every solar eclipse from 1900 to 2200 on a rotating globe from
real ephemeris data.

DAILY PUZZLES
Wordform, Drift, Sudoku, Minesweeper and Flappy Bird each carry a daily
challenge that resets at midnight.

BUILT FOR A REMOTE
The D-pad drives an on-screen pointer and OK taps or drags, so games written for
a mouse work from the sofa. For games where the arrows are the controls, the menu
button hands them straight to the game. Back returns to the catalogue.

No accounts. No sign-up. Nothing to create.

Requires an internet connection: games load from boardgaminghub.com, so they stay
current without an app update.
```

## What to declare about data

The app has no accounts and no login. Game state (scores, settings, saves) stays
in the WebView's local storage on the device.

The pages carry one analytics beacon (`analytics.js`) that POSTs to a
first-party endpoint on each page view: site, path, referrer, screen dimensions,
and any UTM parameters. No cookies, no advertising identifier, nothing that
identifies a person.

**No advertising runs in this app.** The site's AdSense is confined to `/play`,
which the TV catalogue never links to and the in-app navigation cannot reach —
which also keeps the app clear of AdSense's prohibition on serving into a
WebView. The site's privacy policy describes AdSense because it covers the
website too.
