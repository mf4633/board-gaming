What this site does

This site hosts free in-browser games. The games run entirely on your device. Game state (scores, settings, save slots) is stored in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device.

Advertising

This site is supported by Google AdSense. Google and its third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising via aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.com.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland visitors see a consent banner managed by Google's certified CMP before personalized ads are served.

Analytics

This site records anonymous page views (path, referrer, screen size, and optional UTM tags) to measure which games and guides are used. No accounts, cookies, or personal identifiers are stored. AdSense, when enabled on catalog pages only, may additionally collect aggregate impression and click data — see Google's privacy policy.

Data storage on your device

Individual games may store progress in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device. Clearing your browser storage removes it. None of it is transmitted to any server.

The Android apps:

  • Run entirely on your device. Game progress is stored only in the app's local storage and is never transmitted to any server we control.
  • Do not ask for or collect personally identifiable information: no accounts, no logins, no email collection.
  • Do not require an internet connection to play after install (ads need one to load).

The Android apps display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. The AdMob software in the app collects and shares the following with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • Your device's advertising identifier, a resettable ID separate from any account.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • App interactions, such as whether you view or tap an ad.
  • Diagnostics and crash logs, and general device information (model, OS version, language, country).

This data is sent encrypted in transit. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you, and we do not sell it. You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and a "Privacy" button in the app lets you change that choice. For how Google handles this data, see Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

LovedWords, a toddler word app in the same collection, shows no ads and collects no data.

Deleting your data

Game progress lives only on your device: uninstalling an app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it. We hold no account or personal data to delete. Advertising data held by Google can be managed at My Ad Center.

Children

This site and these Android apps are intended for general audiences. Per Google's policies, personalized advertising is not served to users known to be under the age of 13. The Android apps are rated Everyone on Google Play and target users aged 13 and older.
